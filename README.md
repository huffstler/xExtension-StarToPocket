# FreshRSS Star To Pocket (Archived)

A [FreshRSS](https://freshrss.org/) extension that automatically sent the article to Pocket whenever you starred it in FreshRSS.

This enabled my usecase of:

- Starring an article on my phone (using [Read You](https://github.com/Ashinch/ReadYou))
- This extension runs when the star event hits FreshRSS
- The article is saved to my Pocket account (maybe later including tags and such as well)
- I can then access the article on my Kobo E-Reader for a better reading experience.
- I also get a "free backup" of all my starred articles.

With this extension you could star an article to share it with Pocket.
Everything else happened in the background while you can continued reading articles
without any further interruptions.
